Commonquestions

What are your payment terms?

50% deposit before work begins and 50% on final delivery. Invoices come from ZAQ Media, in ETB for local clients and USD for international ones.

How long does a project take?

A brand starter film takes 7–14 business days and a real estate film 5–10. Campaigns run 2–4 weeks, and documentaries and long brand films 4–8 weeks.

How many revisions are included?

Two rounds of revisions on every deliverable. Extra rounds are quoted per project.

How is a project priced?

I price on the outcome, not the hours. Tell me your goal and I'll build a scope and quote around it.

Do you shoot outside Addis Ababa?

Yes. I produce across Ethiopia and East Africa, including location production for international brands. Travel costs are agreed upfront.

Who owns the final film?

You do, once the final payment is made. I keep the right to show the work in my portfolio unless you ask me not to.
